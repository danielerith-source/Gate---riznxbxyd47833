# Hot Doggy Run: what's new

Every few hours the game gets one little improvement. The newest is at the top.

## 14 November 2026

- **Ready, steady, GO!** Every level now starts with a big 3, 2, 1 countdown with little beeps, then a green GO! so you're ready before the hot dog starts running.
- **A better progress bar!** At the top of the screen, a little hot dog now runs along the bar towards a treasure chest, so you can see how far you've got. Blue dots show where the squares are coming up, and gold dots mean a Power Leash!
- **A less crowded road.** Levels 1 to 5 are now nice and simple, with no extras. From level 6, each level has just one surprise, taking turns: banana skins, lost puppies, or the cheeky milk cat. There are fewer coin trails too.
- **New places to run!** The scenery now changes every 5 levels: a sunny meadow (levels 1–5), autumn with orange trees and falling leaves (6–10), a snowy land with snowflakes (11–15), a pink sunset (16–20), and a starry night with the moon (21 and up)!
- **Pause button!** Tap ⏸ to take a break whenever you like. The game also pauses by itself if you put the tablet down or switch to something else. When you tap "Carry on!", a big 3, 2, 1 counts you back in so you're ready.
- **A grand treasure tower!** The tower at the end is now made of real stone bricks, with little glowing windows and castle battlements on top. Golden rays shine from behind the treasure chest, sparkles twinkle round it, clouds float by as you climb, and when you reach the top, confetti rains down!
- **Gold coins on the road!** Shiny spinning coins now sit along the road. Steer through a line of them to grab them all, and when they float up in a high arch, press JUMP to reach the top ones. You keep the coins you grab even if you lose the level. Spend them in the Shop or the Doggie Store!

## 13 November 2026

- **Whoosh!** When you go fast, white wind streaks rush past the sides of the screen, more and more on the speedy levels and near the finish. The hot dog's paws also kick up little puffs of dust as he runs.
- **Faster and faster!** Each level is a little quicker than the one before. Level 1 is nice and gentle, and by level 20 you're zooming!
- **Don't lose all your puppies!** If every puppy gets lost, the hot dog says "Oh no!" and you have to start that level again.
- **Bye bye arrows.** The flying arrows have gone. They were hard to see and not much fun.
- **Lost puppies!** Some little puppies have got lost and are waiting on the road, wagging their tails with a pink heart above them. Run into them and they join your pack, with a happy yip. That's up to 3 extra puppies each time!
- **Shiny glass squares!** The blue squares now look like real see-through glass, with a sparkle of light that sweeps across them. Minus numbers have a red edge so you can spot the bad ones quickly. When you run through a square it smashes into sparkly blue pieces!
- **Watch out for the cheeky cats!** From level 2, naughty cats sit by the road. When you get close they shout "Meow!", throw a carton of milk, and laugh "Hee hee!". If you run through the milk puddle, the hot dog and his puppies slip and slide all over the place. Don't worry, milk never hurts anyone. Steer round it or jump over it!

## 12 November 2026

- **A prettier sky!** There's now a bright sun, fluffy clouds that drift slowly across the sky, and rolling green hills in the distance that shift a little as you steer. Little grass tufts and flowers grow along the sides of the road too.
