Level 1
  • Drag left and right (or use the arrow keys).
  • Run through a blue square to change your crowd: + adds puppies, − takes them away, × multiplies (from level 11). Woof!
  • Nothing can hurt the hot dog, but any puppy hit by an arrow, saw, laser or spikes is lost!
  • Laser walls: run through the green gap. Spikes: wait for them to go down.
  • At the end, every ladder step needs puppies: step 1 needs 1, step 2 needs 2, step 3 needs 3…
  • Reach the top to open the treasure chest!
