Level 1
  • Drag left and right (or use the arrow keys).
  • Run through a blue square to change your crowd: + adds puppies, − takes them away, × multiplies (from level 11). Woof!
  • Nothing can hurt the hot dog, but any puppy hit by a saw, laser, spikes or other trap is lost. Lose them all and you have to start the level again!
  • Laser walls: run through the green gap. Spikes flash red before they rise. The hot dog hops over holes, but puppies fall in!
  • Watch out for swinging hammers and rolling boulders too.
  • Tap JUMP (or press space) to hop over holes, spikes and lasers. The puppies jump too! Saws, hammers and boulders are too tall to jump, so dodge them.
  • POWER LEASH (the gold square): your whole pack is invincible for 5 seconds!
  • Grab dog biscuits and bacon on the road: they make your puppies tougher, so traps miss them.
  • Grab the gold coins on the road. Jump for the high ones!
  • Pick up lost puppies waiting on the road and they join your pack!
  • Watch out for banana skins! Gobble one and you'll spin round and skid. Jump over them!
  • Cheeky cats throw milk on the road. Run through it and everyone slips and slides about! Steer round it or jump over.
  • Spend your coins in the Shop on spare Power Leashes, biscuits and extra puppies, or dress up your hot dog in the Doggie Store!
  • Win a level and the puppies will give you a lick!
  • At the end, every ladder step needs puppies: step 1 needs 1, step 2 needs 2, step 3 needs 3… and on the big levels the top step needs 100!
  • Reach the top to open the treasure chest!
