SUBWAY
MINIONS

Run your minion through the subway. Grab bananas, climb onto the trains and hop from roof to roof. Crash and your minion faints, unless a banana board saves you.

  • Swipe left / right (or ← →): change track
  • Swipe up (or ↑ / Space): jump hurdles and onto trains
  • Swipe down (or ↓): roll under bars
  • Dodge the posts and the fronts of trains
  • Big red trains move towards you and are too tall to climb. Only a banana board can jump you onto them
  • 10 🍌 = 1 banana board (button or B): you float, jump higher, and it's an extra life. Crash and the board disappears but you keep going
  • P / Esc: pause · 🔊: music and sound on/off
