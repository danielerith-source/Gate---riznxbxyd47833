How to play
  • Swipe left / right (or ← →): change track
  • Swipe up (or ↑ / Space): jump hurdles and onto trains
  • Swipe down (or ↓): roll under bars
  • Dodge the posts and the fronts of trains
  • Big red trains move towards you and are too tall to climb. Only a board can jump you onto them
  • 10 🍌 = ride your board (button or B): you float, jump higher, go faster, and it's an extra life
  • Your bananas are saved. Spend them here on new characters and better boards
  • P / Esc: pause · 🔊: music and sound on/off
